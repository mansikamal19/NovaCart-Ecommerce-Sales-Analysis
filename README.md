# NovaCart E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes NovaCart e-commerce sales data to evaluate overall business performance, product and order trends, and customer purchasing behavior.

The analysis uses SQL for business-focused data analysis and Power BI for interactive dashboards and KPI reporting. The objective is to convert transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used
- SQL – Data Analysis and Business Queries
- Power BI – Data Transformation, Data Modeling, DAX, and Dashboard Development

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales: ₹690M
- Total Profit: ₹190M
- Profit Margin: 27.48%
- Total Orders: 30K
- Total Customers: 5K
- Total Products: 22
- Average Order Quantity: 2.00
- Average Order Value: ₹25.57K

## Key Insights
- Electronics contributes approximately 79% of recognized sales, indicating significant revenue concentration in one category.
- Sales performance declines during the second half of the year compared with the first half.
- Order volume is also lower during the second half of the year.
- Revenue is concentrated among a limited number of products.
- Customers purchase approximately 2 units per order on average.
- The dataset contains approximately 2K cancelled orders and 1K returned orders.
- Sales contribution across regions is relatively balanced.
- UPI represents the largest payment-method share at approximately 31%.
- Customers generating the highest sales are not necessarily the customers generating the highest profit.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the drivers behind the decline in sales and order volume during the second half of the year.
- Reduce dependency on the Electronics category by monitoring and developing other product categories.
- Review high-revenue products alongside profitability to prioritize sustainable product performance.
- Analyze cancellation and return patterns to identify opportunities for operational improvement.
- Evaluate customers using both sales and profit rather than revenue alone.
- Continue monitoring regional and payment-method performance for changes in customer behavior.
